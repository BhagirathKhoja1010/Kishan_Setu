# 🌾 KisanSetu

### Farmers Procurement Schedule & Live Status Tracking System

KisanSetu is a smart digital procurement platform designed to help farmers
book mandi procurement slots, receive digital tokens, track their crop
procurement status, and monitor the process from gate entry to payment.

> 🚀 Developed as a Smart India Hackathon (SIH) prototype for problem
> statement **SIH26032 – Farmers Procurement Schedule/Status Tracking App**.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Farmers often face difficulties during crop procurement because of:

- Long waiting queues at procurement centres
- Unclear procurement schedules
- Lack of real-time token/status information
- Manual paperwork
- Difficulty tracking weighing and quality checks
- Lack of transparency in payment status

KisanSetu aims to provide a single digital platform for managing and
tracking the procurement journey.

---
